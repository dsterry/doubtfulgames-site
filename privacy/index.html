Privacy

Doubtful Games respects your privacy. Project: Kept is designed to be played without creating an account or providing personal information.

Information collected by the game

Project: Kept does not collect, transmit, or share personal information or gameplay data. The game does not contain advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, social-media integrations, or third-party telemetry services.

Game progress, settings, scores, and other gameplay information are stored only on your device. Doubtful Games does not receive this information. Removing the game may also remove its locally stored data, subject to your device’s operating-system and backup behavior.

Apple services

When you download or purchase Project: Kept through the App Store, Apple may process information about the transaction, your device, and your use of the App Store. Apple may also collect crash reports, diagnostics, or aggregated usage information according to your device settings and Apple’s policies.

Apple may make certain diagnostic or aggregated information available to Doubtful Games. If we access this information, we use it only to investigate technical problems and understand the game’s overall performance. We do not use it to identify or track individual players.

Doubtful Games does not receive or store your payment information. Apple’s collection and processing of information is governed by Apple’s own privacy policies.

Website

The Doubtful Games website does not use advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or non-essential cookies.

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including visitors’ IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service. This processing is governed by GitHub’s privacy policies.

Contact and support

If you contact Doubtful Games, we receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address and the contents of your request. We use this information only to respond and provide support. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

Support correspondence is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request and maintain appropriate business records. You may ask us to delete your correspondence, subject to any legal obligations requiring its retention.

Children’s privacy

Project: Kept does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other players.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the game or website changes. If Project: Kept begins collecting analytics or other information, this policy will be updated before that collection begins. The effective date above will identify the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact support@doubtfulgames.com.
