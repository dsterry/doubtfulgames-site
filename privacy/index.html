Privacy

This policy explains how Doubtful Games handles information when you play Project: Kept, visit doubtfulgames.com, or contact us.

Game data and permissions

Project: Kept has no account or sign-in requirement. The game saves your progress, scores, earned frames, and settings on your device so you can resume play and keep your preferences. The game does not send that information to Doubtful Games or provide cloud saves.

The game has no ads or built-in analytics. It does not collect personal or usage data, track you across apps or websites, or send player data to third parties, including advertisers, analytics providers, or AI services. It does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photo library.

Photo Mode hides the game’s controls so you can take a screenshot using your device. Any screenshot you take or share is handled by your device and the service you choose.

To remove the game and its saved data from your iPhone or iPad, touch and hold the Project: Kept icon on the Home Screen. Tap Remove App, choose Delete App, and confirm with Delete. Offload App keeps your saved data. Any separate device backups are managed through your device or backup service. See Apple’s instructions for deleting an app.

Apple services

Apple processes App Store downloads and purchases under Apple’s Privacy Policy. Apple may provide us with aggregate download and sales reports, which we use to understand the game’s distribution. Doubtful Games does not receive or store your payment details.

If you opt in to analytics sharing through Apple, Apple may make crash reports and aggregate or otherwise non-identifying usage statistics available to Doubtful Games. We use reports we access to evaluate the game’s reliability and performance. We do not use them to identify or track players.

To stop sharing diagnostics with app developers, open Settings, tap Privacy & Security, then tap Analytics & Improvements. Turn off Share With App Developers if you see that switch. If your device uses one switch for analytics sharing, turn off Share iPhone Analytics, Share iPhone & Watch Analytics, or Share iPad Analytics, whichever appears. Your choice does not affect access to the game. See Apple’s guide to app analytics sharing. For data held by Apple, use Apple’s Data and Privacy portal.

Website

The Doubtful Games website does not use advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or non-essential cookies. Its social links open the platforms you choose to visit. Those platforms handle information under their own privacy policies.

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including visitors’ IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service. This processing is governed by the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Contact and support

Contacting us is optional. When you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any details or attachments you send. Our email service processes the correspondence to deliver and store it. We use the information to respond and provide support. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request and maintain appropriate business records. We retain any copies of Apple diagnostic reports only as long as needed to evaluate the game’s reliability and performance.

Privacy choices and deletion requests

To request deletion of personal information we hold, including support correspondence, or to withdraw consent for any processing that relies on it, email support@doubtfulgames.com. Tell us which information or correspondence your request concerns. We may verify your request and retain information when required by law.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal information you provide when contacting us. These may include the rights to access, correct, or delete your information; restrict or object to its use; receive a portable copy; or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

You may exercise an applicable right at the same email address. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

Children’s privacy

Project: Kept does not collect personal information from children during play. If you believe a child sent us personal information by email, contact us so we can review and delete it as appropriate.

Changes to this policy

We update this policy before changing the information Project: Kept collects. The date above identifies the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact support@doubtfulgames.com.
