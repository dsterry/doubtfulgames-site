Project: Kept

Game support

Learn the controls, find your way around Settings, and get help with Project: Kept.

How to play

  1. Check your goals. Look at the objectives above the board to see what the puzzle asks you to collect or clear.
  2. Connect matching pieces. Drag through two or more pieces of the same color, moving up, down, left, or right. Lift your finger to clear the pieces in your path.
  3. Close a loop. Bring your path back around to a piece you already connected before lifting your finger. Loops can clear matching pieces across the board and pieces or obstacles inside the loop.
  4. Rotate the board. When the curved arrow is visible, drag from it to turn the board, then release. A different orientation can open new paths.

Complete the puzzle’s goals to earn a check mark. More efficient solutions can earn additional check marks.

Frequently asked questions

Can I undo a move?

Use Undo to take back your last move once per attempt. To start the puzzle over, choose Restart in Settings. You can retry as often as you like.

Can I make colors easier to distinguish?

Turn on Show Symbols in Settings to add a symbol to each color.

Can the game remind me to take a break?

Open Settings and choose an interval under Break Reminder, or select Off.

What is Photo Mode?

Choose Photo Mode in Settings to hide the controls while you take a screenshot of a puzzle or its framed preview. Tap the screen to return to the game.

Where is my progress stored?

Your progress, settings, and scores are stored on your device. Project: Kept does not use accounts or cloud saves.

Does the game collect gameplay data?

No. Project: Kept does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party telemetry. See the privacy policy for details.

Get help

For help with Project: Kept, email support@doubtfulgames.com. A brief description of your question, your device model, and the game version help us respond. Share only the personal information needed for your request.
