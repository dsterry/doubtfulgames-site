Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress stored?

Your progress, settings, and scores are stored locally on your device. Project: Kept does not currently use accounts or cloud saves.

Does the game collect gameplay data?

No. The current version does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party telemetry. See the privacy policy for complete details.

How can I report a problem?

Email support@doubtfulgames.com. Please include the device you are using, the version of the game, and a description of what happened. Avoid including personal information that is not needed to investigate the issue.
