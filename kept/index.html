Project: Kept

Project: Kept is a gentle puzzle journey about connection, transformation, and a sense of place.

Connect matching colors, make loops, and discover your own way through handcrafted puzzles. Calm by design: subtle motion, rich color, and nothing shouting for your attention. Take a break and train your brain.

Features

  • Free to play, with no ads
  • Play at your own pace, with unlimited retries
  • Low pressure: no win streaks, chores, or fake urgency
  • Difficult but fair: puzzles are never designed to sell you the solution
  • Change the board, not just the pieces, to find new solutions
  • Optional colorblind-friendly palettes
  • A growing collection of handcrafted puzzles with new mechanics and challenges
