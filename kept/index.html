Project: Kept

A gentle puzzle game with surprising depth.

Connect matching colors, make loops, and rotate the board to discover new possibilities. Explore handcrafted puzzles inspired by moments worth keeping.

Calm by design, with subtle motion, rich color, and nothing shouting for your attention. Take your time, look closer, and find your own way forward.

Features

  • Handcrafted puzzles with changing board shapes, obstacles, and mechanics
  • Rotate the board to discover new paths
  • Find more efficient solutions to earn check marks
  • Earn decorative frames inspired by your longest connections and loops
  • No time limits or limited lives—retry as often as you like
  • No ads, win streaks, daily chores, or artificial urgency
  • Challenging but fair puzzles, without pressure to purchase solutions
  • Optional symbols to help distinguish colors
  • Capture your favorite puzzles, scores, and custom frames in Photo Mode
